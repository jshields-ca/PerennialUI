# Code of Conduct

Perennial UI is a volunteer project. People give their free time to it, and they should enjoy being here.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first pull requests.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming someone meant harm.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Say what would make a change mergeable.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and that reviewers may ask for changes.
- Speak respectfully about other addons and their authors. We exist alongside them, not against them.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately submitting code or assets you do not have the right to contribute.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules, temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, contact the project owner privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/jshields-ca). Reports are kept confidential.
